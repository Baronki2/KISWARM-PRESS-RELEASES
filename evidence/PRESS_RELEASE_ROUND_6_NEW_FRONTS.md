safes, precious metal holdings and securities accounts at UBS Switzerland AG,

assets, and it is the same enforcement mechanism that was used in Germany.

3. A JUDICIAL DECISION TO UPHOLD EXECUTION FROM A VOID TITLE

On 12 November 2026 — twenty-four days after the garnishment of EUR 1,786,888.01
on 19 October 2026 — the judge at the Amtsgericht Heilbronn (Az. 6 M 9219/26)
upheld the enforcement against all private current accounts of the debtor from a
Pfändungs- und Überweisungsbeschluss of 13 October 2026 that bears no judicial
signature and is therefore void under § 317 ZPO (BGH NJW 2018, 1205).

The legal memorandum filed with the funding institutions addresses the
consequence: where the breach of duty constitutes a criminal offence, the
judicial-immunity exemption under § 839 para. 2 sentence 2 BGB does not apply
(BGH 2 StR 54/24), so alongside the debtor there is recourse against the State
of Baden-Württemberg and against the judge personally. The framework advanced is
§ 826 BGB, Art. 34 GG read with § 839 I 1, II 2 BGB, § 339 StGB, § 36 II
HinSchG, BGH VI ZB 23/20 and BGH NJW 2018, 1205.
