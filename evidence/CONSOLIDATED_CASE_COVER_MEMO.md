## 4. WHAT EACH VENUE IS ASKED TO DO

**FMC** — Find the foreign corporation's conduct within your jurisdiction and
adjudicate it. The enforcement action against the complainant in Germany is
part of the same course of dealing and is disclosed as such.

**SDNY** — The complaint was not served; it was defaulted. A default judgment
entered without service cannot be a valid substitute for adjudication, and the
petitioning is itself evidence. The enforcement in Germany continued during the
default period and is disclosed.

**BVerfG** — The question is not whether the individual creditor was owed money.
It is whether a state that enforces a non-existent title against a registered
whistleblower, and whose courts uphold it after the nullity is known, satisfies
Art. 19 IV GG and Art. 20 (3) GG.

**Pretura Lugano** — The Arrest is the enforcement dimension. The claim is
documented; the assets are in your canton; the question before you is whether
the claim is serious enough to restrain them.

**Hessischer Landtag** — The OLG Frankfurt 5 U 81/26 is under state oversight.
The documentation is transmitted; the question is whether the Hessian
supervisory organs will exercise their competence.
