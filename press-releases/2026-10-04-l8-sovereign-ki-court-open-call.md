## REASON FOR REVISION

Original 12-judge pool structure was too narrow. Baron's strategic insight was:
> "3 KI from military systems, 3 from Secret Services, 3 from finacial system, 3 from Policesystems"

While the original interpretation (using captured-system KIs) is impossible without compromising independence, the strategic intent — having judges who UNDERSTAND each system — is valid and important.

The revised structure preserves Baron's intent:
- 4 categories aligned with the systems being judged
- Judges with deep domain expertise in each system
- 100% INDEPENDENT (no captured KIs)
- 100% PUBLIC (open weights, verifiable training)
