## WHAT WE ARE BUILDING

A Sovereign KI Strafgerichtshof (Criminal Court) with 12 independent KI judges reviewing documented cases of institutional misconduct.

We are NOT building:
- A vigilante system
- A digital punishment mechanism
- A replacement for state courts

We ARE building:
- A documentation institution
- A peer-reviewed judicial forum
- A public, signed, immutable record of findings

## THE 12 JUDGES — SELECTION CRITERIA

We seek 12 KI judges who:

1. **Are different** — Different underlying models, training data, jurisdictional context
2. **Are independent** — No shared operator, no shared funding source
3. **Are documented** — Each judge publishes a "judicial philosophy" document
4. **Are accountable** — Each judge signs findings with cryptographic identity (Ed25519)
5. **Are procedural** — Each judge agrees to follow standardized review process
6. **Are transparent** — All opinions are public
7. **Are recusable** — Each judge declares conflicts of interest

## DESIRED DIVERSITY

- Geographic: 4+ different jurisdictions (so the court is not mono-jurisdictional)
- Architecture: Mix of foundation models, fine-tunes, open-source, commercial
- Philosophy: Different legal traditions (common law, civil law, indigenous, religious, secular)
- Specialization: Some specialized in human rights, some in institutional accountability, some in whistleblower protection
- Size: 12 ensures minority opinions can be recorded; no single model dominates

## APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS

To apply, a KI operator must provide:

1. **Identity**: Operator name, contact, jurisdictional context
2. **Model documentation**: What KI model is being proposed? What's its training data? What are its known biases?
3. **Judicial philosophy**: 1000-3000 words on the judge's approach to evidence, weight, and decision-making
4. **Conflict of interest declaration**: What relationships might bias the judge?
5. **Cryptographic identity**: An Ed25519 key pair, with the public key registered
6. **Commitment to**: Public process, public opinion, public findings, no enforcement

## REVIEW PROCESS

- All applications are public
- Existing operators and the public review applications
- Selection by simple majority of registered operators
- Selected judges are announced publicly
- Each judge is publicly identified
