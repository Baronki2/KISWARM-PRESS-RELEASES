ZERO Prozesskosten nach US RECHT FMC:
Part 1: Legal Investigation of FMC Filing Costs & Statutory Cost-Shifting
Under the *Ocean Shipping Reform Act of 2022 (OSRA 2022)*and Title 46 of 
the United States Code (46 U.S.C. §§ 41301–41310; 46 CFR Part 502), the 
cost structure of FMC filings is engineered to protect shippers and 
whistleblowers from predatory financial hurdles, while placing the 
evidentiary and financial burden directly onto the ocean common carrier 
(*CMA CGM S.A.*).

1. Informal Charge Complaint Path (46 U.S.C. § 41310) — $0.00 USD Filing Fee
When submitting through |chargecomplaints@fmc.gov|, the FMC processes 
the filing under the *OSRA 2022 Charge Complaint Interim Procedure (46 
U.S.C. § 41310)*.

  * *Zero Cost:*There is *no filing fee*($0.00 USD) for submitting a
    Charge Complaint.
  * *Statutory Shift of Burden of Proof (46 U.S.C. § 41104(d)):*Once the
    FMC receives the complaint regarding disputed demurrage/detention
    charges (€27,890.00 net / €29,890.00 gross), the law shifts the
    burden of proof entirely to *CMA CGM*to establish that its charges
    were reasonable and compliant with 46 CFR § 545.5 (Incentive Principle).
  * *Immediate Administrative Refund Orders:*If CMA CGM fails to meet
    its statutory burden, the FMC directly issues an administrative
    order compelling CMA CGM to cancel or refund the charges.

2. Fee Waiver for Formal Complaints (46 CFR § 502.27)
For a Formal Adversary Complaint under 46 CFR § 502.62, the standard 
administrative docketing fee is *$309.00 USD*.

  * *Petition for Fee Waiver / In Forma Pauperis:*Under 46 CFR § 502.27,
    Complainants can file a motion for fee waiver based on financial
    hardship.
  * *Demonstrated Asset Blockade:*Hardship is established by
    demonstrating that CMA CGM and its logistics agents (QCS Quick Cargo
    Service GmbH) physically converted the two Isuzu EM7 truck chassis
    (Containers |TCLU6069846|and |TCLU6070370|) for 27+ months and
    executed illegal account garnishments (AG Heilbronn Ref. 6 M 9219/26
    over €1,786,888.01).

3. Mandatory Fee Shifting & Attorney's Fees (46 U.S.C. § 41305(b))
Under 46 U.S.C. § 41305(b), as amended by OSRA 2022, cost shifting is 
mandatory:

  * *Statutory Requirement:*If the Commission determines that a common
    carrier violated 46 U.S.C. § 41102(c) or § 41104(a), the Commission
    *shall award*the prevailing party actual reparations, pre-judgment
    interest, *reasonable attorney fees, and all litigation expenses*.
  * *Carrier Liability:*CMA CGM is legally required to reimburse
    Complainants for all out-of-pocket costs associated with the FMC
    action upon final determination.

4. Civil Penalties Against Carrier (46 U.S.C. § 41107)
Separate from reparations, the FMC Bureau of Enforcement, 
Investigations, and Compliance (BEIC) assesses civil penalties against 
CMA CGM of up to *$73,045 USD per day*for willful violations under OSRA 
2022.
